Privacy Policy

Starrypad · last updated 29 August 2026

Starrypad collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network access of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything the app makes or is given:

  • Sounds you record with the microphone, and audio taken from a video you choose.
  • Your kit: which sound is on which pad, levels, panning, tuning, trims and names.
  • Settings such as tempo, master volume and the MIDI layout you have taught it.

These are written to the app's own storage on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you tap “Record from the microphone”, and only to record the sound you are pointing it at. The recording is written to the app's storage and played back from a pad. Nothing is transmitted, and nothing is listened to at any other time.

Photos and videos

When you take the sound from a video, iOS shows its own picker, which runs outside the app. Starrypad never gets access to your photo library — only to the single video you pick, and only for as long as it takes to read the first thirty seconds of its audio.

Diagnostic log

The app keeps a short written record of what it did — when it started, when the audio engine failed or was interrupted, when a controller was plugged in, when an export succeeded — in one file on your phone. It holds no content: no sample names, no pad names, nothing you typed.

It is never uploaded. It travels only as an attachment on an email you write yourself from the app's contact button, it is switched on by a control you can switch off, and you can read every line of it before you send it.

Data sent to us or to anyone else

None. There is no server, no third-party SDK, no crash reporter and no tracking. The app works identically with the phone in aeroplane mode.

Children

Starrypad is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
